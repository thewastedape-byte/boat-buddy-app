**Boat Buddy AI — Your Expert Marine Mechanic, Always On Call**

Whether you're a weekend boater, marine technician, or marina operator — Boat Buddy AI gives you expert-level marine knowledge right from your phone. No more guessing at engine problems or waiting on hold for a shop.

---

🔧 **AI Marine Diagnostics**
Describe any symptom in plain English and get a real diagnosis — like texting an experienced mechanic. Boat Buddy understands outboard motors, inboard engines, sterndrives, marine electrical systems, bilge pumps, and more.

📷 **Photo Analysis**
Send a photo of your engine, wiring, or bilge. Our AI identifies parts, spots issues, and walks you through the fix — right from the image.

📖 **Service Manual Search**
Get exact torque specs, part numbers, valve clearances, and fluid capacities — pulled from real manufacturer PDFs. Yamaha, Mercury, Volvo Penta, Yanmar, Mercruiser, Honda, Suzuki, and more.

📐 **Marine System Diagrams**
25+ professional marine wiring, fuel, cooling, and electrical system diagrams — always available offline.

📄 **Work Orders & Invoices**
Save diagnoses, log repairs, and generate branded PDF invoices for customers. Built for working marine techs.

🚢 **Vessel Management**
Track your vessel's service history, maintenance schedule, insurance documents, and specs — all in one place.

🏗️ **Yard & Marina Tools** *(Captain & Admiral plans)*
Run your entire marine service operation: job board, parts inventory with barcode scanning, customer database, team management, and real-time group chat for your crew.
